Understanding 2026 US Etsy Seller Deductions

Etsy collects three primary fees on each completed transaction:

  • Listing Fee: $0.20 per item listing for four months or until sold.
  • Transaction Fee: 6.5% of the total amount charged to the buyer (item + shipping).
  • Payment Processing: 3% plus $0.25 flat charge per transaction in the United States.
